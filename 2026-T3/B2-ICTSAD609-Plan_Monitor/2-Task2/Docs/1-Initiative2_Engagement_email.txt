Dear Noah and Victorine,

I am writing to invite your participation in the initial stakeholder engagement meeting for the Strategic Hardware Partnerships and Outsourced Distribution initiative. This is one of two business analysis initiatives proposed in response to Worlducation's 2020 supply chain disruptions and financial pressures.

The 1,500-tablet delivery shortfall in 2020, caused by the Hong Kong factory closure and escalating geopolitical tensions, demonstrated the risk of reliance on a single manufacturing source. Management is assessing a model under which qualified external partners source and distribute hardware while Worlducation retains ownership of its proprietary AI software and content.

Your operational and commercial input is required to ensure that the initiative is practical, operationally sound and commercially sustainable.

The proposed meeting will address the following:

1. Current supply chain and fulfilment issues that the initiative must address.
2. Requirements for integration of partner systems with the ERP platform, including order routing, delivery tracking and software pre-installation.
3. Partner selection criteria, including delivery timeframes, quality standards and customer service requirements.
4. Impacts on sales processes, customer commitments and order fulfilment workflows.
5. Workforce transition considerations for staff involved in manufacturing coordination.

The meeting is expected to run for approximately 30 minutes. Please provide your availability for the week of 25 January 2026, and I will confirm the meeting invitation.

All input and observations will be recorded in the Stakeholder Feedback Register in accordance with Worlducation's Documentation Policy. The information will be used to inform the business analysis plan and requirements documentation for this initiative.

Please contact me if you have any questions before the meeting.
