Dear Noah and Victorine,

I am writing to formally invite your participation in the initial stakeholder engagement for the Strategic Hardware Partnerships and Outsourced Distribution initiative, which has been identified as one of two key business analysis initiatives in response to Worlducation's 2020 supply chain disruptions and financial pressures.

As you are aware, the delivery shortfall of 1,500 tablets in 2020, caused by the Hong Kong factory closure and escalating geopolitical tensions, has highlighted the critical risk of relying on a single manufacturing source. The management team is evaluating transitioning to a model where hardware is sourced and distributed through qualified external partners, while Worlducation retains ownership of its proprietary AI software and content.

As key internal stakeholders with direct operational and commercial responsibilities, your input is essential to ensuring this initiative is planned and executed in a way that is practical, operationally sound, and commercially sustainable.

I would like to meet with you to discuss the following:

1. Current operational pain points in the supply chain and fulfilment process that the initiative must address.
2. Requirements for integrating partner systems with our ERP platform (order routing, delivery tracking, software pre-installation).
3. Operational criteria for partner selection, including delivery timelines, quality standards, and customer service expectations.
4. Sales process impacts, including how the partnership model will affect existing customer commitments and order fulfilment workflows.
5. Workforce transition considerations for staff currently involved in manufacturing coordination.

The meeting is expected to run for approximately 30 minutes. Please reply with your availability for the week of January 25, 2026, and I will confirm the meeting invitation.

All input and observations from this meeting will be documented in the Stakeholders Feedback Register in accordance with Worlducation's Documentation Policy and will be used to inform the business analysis plan and requirements documentation for this initiative.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions prior to the meeting.
