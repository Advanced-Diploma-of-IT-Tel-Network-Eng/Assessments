# Stakeholder Feedback Register

**Project:** Worlducation  
**Initiative:** 1

**Date:** 2 February 2026

**Person Providing Feedback:** Operations Team Manager – Noah Jones

**Department Receiving Feedback:** Senior Management and Founders /Professional Services

**Comments/Observations:**

While the SaaS model offers an innovative alternative to supply chain disruptions, there are significant operational concerns that require further analysis.

I am concerned about the feasibility of the human capital transition; our current team possesses technical expertise in hardware that may not be immediately transferable to the B2C model, which poses risks to talent retention and job stability.

Likewise, it is imperative to assess whether the savings in manufacturing will be offset by new operating expenses (direct marketing, payment gateways, and remote technical support).

To ensure that we recover our 10% net profit margin, we need assurances that customer acquisition costs in a B2C environment will not compromise our short-term liquidity or the project's sustainability by 2026.

**Actions Required:**

Provide more details to all operations teams, with a breakdown of:

1. Workforce Impact Analysis: Conduct a detailed study on job redundancy and develop an upskilling plan for operations personnel.
2. B2C Cost Audit: Present a comparative breakdown of manufacturing savings versus the projected increase in marketing and digital distribution costs.
3. Risk Mitigation Plan: Define support protocols for the B2C model that do not require an initial investment that would compromise 2026 liquidity.

# Stakeholder Feedback Register

**Project:** Worlducation  
**Initiative:** 1

**Date:** 25 January 2026

**Person Providing Feedback:** Operations & Business Analyst – Luiz Ricardo

**Department Receiving Feedback:** Senior Management and Founders /Professional Services

**Comments/Observations:**

Based on our 2020 financial analysis, it is clear that our current hardware model is unsustainable. Although we projected revenue of $55 million, the drop in net profit to $5 million and a negative cash flow of $3 million confirm that operating costs and our reliance on manufacturing in Hong Kong are reducing profitability.

The delivery of only 500 of the 2,000 tablets planned demonstrates that supply chain disruption is the critical risk. I fully support the transition to a Cloud-First SaaS and B2C model, as it eliminates our dependence on physical assets and external factories. This solution will not only stabilize cash flow by reducing inventory investment but will also allow us to scale software delivery directly to the end customer without the logistical delays we currently face.

I understand that the transition will be a significant but necessary process to continue the business.

**Actions Required:**

The Operations & Analyst team is providing support in the following areas:

1. Interdepartmental Collaboration: The operations team is available to share historical customer data and ensure a smooth migration to the SaaS environment.
2. Commitment Review: We are evaluating how the B2C model can fulfill outstanding orders for the 1,500 undelivered tablets by providing immediate access to the digital platform.

# Stakeholder Feedback Register

**Project:** Worlducation  
**Initiative:** 2

**Date:** 1 February 2026

**Person Providing Feedback:** Sales Team Manager – Victorine Satore

**Department Receiving Feedback:** Senior Management and Founders /Professional Services

**Comments/Observations:**

There is insufficient clarity regarding the practical implementation of this transition. My sales team has historically based its strategy on the physical distribution of tablets; to date, the sales team requires clarification regarding how the sales processes will transform when moving from a tangible product to a software as a service (SaaS).

Furthermore, there is significant uncertainty about the future of current roles. It's unclear what will happen to the staff who currently manage hardware production and supply chain. Without a detailed roadmap on how these employees will integrate into the new B2C model, or if their roles will disappear, team morale is at risk. We need to understand how the absence of the physical product in the sales offering will be compensated for, and what training sales and distribution employees will receive to avoid becoming obsolete during this change.

**Actions Required:**

1. Provide the Definition of New Roles
2. Conduct SaaS Training Workshops
3. Create a Transition Communication Plan for all employees

**Comments/Observations:**

Regarding the Strategic Hardware Partnerships, I agree that it is a necessary step to bypass the bottleneck at our Hong Kong facility. We cannot afford another delay like the one that left 1,500 tablets undelivered in 2020. However, adding third-party manufacturers creates a significant complexity for the Operations team in terms of ERP integration.

If we don't automate the order routing from our system to the new partners, the Operations team will face an unmanageable manual workload. Also, we must strictly enforce the New Supplier Policy, ensuring partners agree to the 30-day minimum payment terms so Finance approves the orders without delays.

**Actions Required:**

1. ERP Integration Mapping: The IT Professional must document how the partner's manufacturing system will communicate with our current ERP.
2. Compliance Check: Ensure all potential partners undergo the formal background check and bank details verification before signing contracts.

My main concern with outsourcing the hardware is quality control and customer satisfaction. We have over 550 schools expecting the same high-quality product they purchased before the pandemic. Although we lose a $200 margin per unit, it is better than losing customers entirely.
