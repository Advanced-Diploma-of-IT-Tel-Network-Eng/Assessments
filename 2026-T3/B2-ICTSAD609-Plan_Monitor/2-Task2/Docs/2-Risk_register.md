## Outcomes of Stakeholder Meetings

### 1. Cloud-First SaaS and B2C Model Meeting

**Participants:** Noah Jones, Victorine Satore and Luiz Ricardo

Stakeholder attitudes towards the initiative were mixed. Luiz Ricardo provided analytical support because the initiative addresses the logistics disruption and removes the hardware dependency that caused the 1,500-tablet delivery shortfall in 2020.

Noah Jones and Victorine Satore identified operational concerns regarding workforce transition to the B2C model, the viability of new sales roles and the effect of customer acquisition costs on short-term liquidity.

**Risks Identified**

1. **Talent retention risk:** Operations and sales teams may resist the change or leave the organisation because hardware-focused skills may become less relevant in a software-focused model.
2. **Financial expectation risk:** Operational stakeholders may expect the SaaS model to restore margins immediately, despite the possibility of short-term cash-flow pressure during the B2C transition.

**Mitigation Plan**

1. Prepare a Workforce Impact Analysis and provide training workshops to develop digital operations and SaaS sales capabilities.
2. Present a comparative cost audit to clarify the financial projections and transition impacts.
