Dear Senior Management and Founders,

I am writing to request approval for the proposed Cloud-First SaaS and B2C Model. The initiative is intended to address Worlducation's current operational and financial challenges.

Revised financial projections for 2020 indicate a mismatch between increasing market demand and current manufacturing and supply chain capacity. Continued reliance on physical manufacturing has exposed the organisation to supply chain disruption, increased operating costs, reduced profitability and negative cash flow.

The proposal is based on existing information and stakeholder feedback. It supports service continuity, improves supply chain resilience and enables the organisation to scale without further manufacturing constraints.

The initiative aligns with Worlducation's priorities for performance, profitability, accountability and long-term sustainability. It also supports the organisation's mission to expand access to education by extending reach beyond conventional school-based delivery models.

The following risks have been identified:

- Workforce transition.
- Financial sustainability.
- Cybersecurity.

These risks will be managed through governance controls, detailed planning and phased decision-making if approval is granted.

I request approval to proceed with detailed planning and assessment in accordance with Worlducation's policies and applicable legal requirements.

Please contact me if additional information is required to support the decision.
