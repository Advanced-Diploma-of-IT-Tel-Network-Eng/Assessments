1. Outline the purpose and scope of business process design.
The purpose of business process design is to develop an effective approach to processes within a company.

The scope of business process design focuses on defining tasks, which are then documented within the workflow in accordance with company requirements to ensure that all requirements are met.

4. Discuss the implications of technology connectivity for businesses.
The implications of a company’s technological connectivity, I can be categorized based on:
- Functionality: performance, reliability, design
- Security: cybersecurity and mitigation
- Compatibility: dependence on other technologies, laws, and regulations

Therefore, for instance, the implementation of a new internal network must allow for relatively easy integration into the company’s existing technological ecosystem.

7. Explain two methods that can be used to calculate the implementation costs of a business solution. 
- ROI: ROI (Return on Investment) is a method that uses the relationship between revenue and costs to determine if an investment is financially viable. Direct costs (software/hardware/services) and indirect costs are used to determine if the costs are too high. This allows you to calculate whether implementing a business solution is feasible.
- Cost Analysis: It is used to calculate all the costs of a project, taking into account:
	- fixed costs
	- variable costs
	- direct costs
	- indirect costs

This way, the total cost of implementing a business solution can be determined, and a decision can be made about its feasibility.
