#Activities
Complete the following activities:

**1\. Carefully read the following:**
This project requires you to develop technical and related requirements that will enable business solutions to be implemented in an organisation. This project can be based on the case study business in the ICT Simulation Pack or you may like to base this on your own business, or a business you are currently working for or are familiar with. If you do not choose the case study, It is important that you can access information about the business, including information about their current IT infrastructure, their goals and objectives for this and future business operations, as well as their business and customer relationship model and procedures. Speak to your assessor to get approval if you want to base this on your chosen business, one you work for or you own business. You will collect evidence for this unit by using the Project Portfolio. The steps you need to take are outlined below.

**2\. Planning**
Make sure you are familiar with the business you are basing this assessment on and have read through the necessary background information. For the case study business, you will need to read all of the documents included in the ICT Simulation Pack. If it’s your own business or a business where you are working or are familiar with, it’s important at this step that you have your business or case study approved by your assessor, and have access to all relevant documentation. Complete _Page 4_ of your Project Portfolio for this unit. Read through the requirements of Sections 1–6_​_ of your _Project Portfolio_. 
**3\. Review business documentation**

_If you are completing the case study, read the following (otherwise skip to the instructions below):_Ahead of your meeting, review the information in the Simulation Pack – specifically the Strategic Plan. Consider what you have learned about King Edward VII College and its goals and objectives, and think about how the organisation’s IT infrastructure could play a role in impacting the way in which the organisation can meet these goals. Consider the impact of a poor infrastructure on the clients (students), the staff and their delivery of services to their clients. _If you are completing the assessment using your chosen business:_Review the organisation’s strategic plan, its procedures and other relevant information. You should also look over its website and any promotional/marketing materials to gain a picture of its business model. Take notes on what you have learned about the business and answer the questions in Section 1 of your _Project Portfolio_. Feel free to attach any documentation or screenshots that may help to remind you of things as you progress through the assessment.When you take notes in Section 1, you will also need to document your thoughts on:

*   the legislation, standards and procedures that will need to be considered for the proposed business solutions
    
*   the business model 
    
*   the types of hardware, software and network requirements that the organisation should have in regards to its business solutions 
    
*   what you consider would be the interface requirements of the organisation, based on your understanding of its end users and external parties
    
*   security protocols. 
    

You will gather more information in the next step, when you meet with the relevant contact at the organisation. Your notes will give you a basis on which to discuss.Submit your thoughts to the relevant organisation contact:

*   If you are completing this task for your chosen business, you should submit your findings in a format suitable to the organisation (this could be in a report, an email or even attaching the table from Section 1). 
    
*   If you are completing the case study business, submit your Project Portfolio to your assessor, who will play the role of Alex Garcia, the CEO. Request their approval on your current findings. 
    

**4\. Gather information about user needs.**
Meet with the relevant contact (or contacts) at the organisation to gather more information about its technical specifications and needs. _If you are completing the case study, go to page 23 of your Simulation Pack. Your assessor will play the role of Alex. If you are completing the assessment using your chosen business, meet with relevant personnel. You will need to submit evidence of your meeting by recording. Make sure you get the person’s permission to record (preferably video evidence)._During the meeting you should:

*   analyse the information you are provided 
    
*   ask appropriate and thoughtful questions to gather further information as necessary 
    
*   demonstrate effective communication skills 
    
*   use the above skills to start building a rapport with your contact
    
*   identify three business solutions, one of which should consider ICT security
    
*   request details on budget and timelines.
    

Make sure you take additional notes. Place these in Section 2 of your Project Portfolio. 

**5\. Research, analyse and report on business solution requirements.**
By this stage you will have learned enough to document the technical requirements for the three business solutions identified.  Complete Section 3_​_ of your _Project Portfolio_.When you complete Section 3, you need to report on the business solution requirements by:

*   outlining the requirements of the business, their business model, needs of end users and external parties 
    
*   noting the legislation, standards and procedures that must be considered 
    
*   documenting the three business solutions (one must be related to security)
    
*   documenting the technical specifications relevant to the three business solutions.
    

You can present your information in any format, as long as it provides the above information. For example, you may like to develop a user needs analysis or write a report that is structured in clear and logical parts. Your report must be submitted to the person you met with for their approval. Request them to provide approval in writing and attach it to Section 3 of your portfolio. Note: if you receive any feedback from your contact, you will need to address it and seek approval again. Attach the revised version and the feedback received to your Portfolio. Complete Section 3 of your Project Portfolio. Submit your Project Portfolio to your assessor for approval.

**6\. Develop an impact statement.**
You are now to complete Section 4_​_ of your _Project Portfolio_.When you complete Section 4, you need to develop an impact statement that provides an analysis and reports on:

*   suitable software solutions 
    
*   suitable hardware and networking solutions as applicable 
    
*   applicable security protocols
    
*   processes within the business’s operations that will need to change 
    
*   analyses the impact of changes on the business’s value and supply chain.
    

In order to do this, you will need to complete a number of steps:

*   conduct research, which should include:
    
    *   reviewing policies and procedures, which will help you identify processes for change
        
    *   researching the technical requirements
        
*   meet with staff/personnel who will be impacted by such changes and discuss their concerns (for case study students, this will be your assessor). 
    

You should analyse a variety of technical options. Cite the sources you used when researching technical information and explain how your research assisted you in coming to the best recommendations. Meet with at least one other person who will be impacted by the changes and discuss any concerns they may have. It is important to remember that you need to stick by your initial recommendations and remember there is a budget and timeline. If this contact has requests or concerns that may not be possible, be prepared to negotiate something that would be more appropriate. If you are completing this task using your chosen business, you will need to again record the interaction (seek permission). Submit this recording as part of your portfolio. During the meeting you should demonstrate effective communication skills and build rapport with this person to get information from them that will help you complete your report.Make sure you take notes. Attach these to your Project Portfolio. Complete Section 4 of your Project Portfolio.Submit your Project Portfolio to your assessor for approval.

**7\. Develop a plan for implementation.**
You are now to complete Section 5_​_ of your _Project Portfolio_.This will include:

*   setting up the hardware and software solutions for testing
    
*   documenting performance benchmarks 
    
*   testing and recording outcomes and comparing against benchmarks 
    
*   meeting with at least one person (preferably a manager) to identify training needs
    
*   using the information gathered to develop a training needs analysis 
    
*   develop a schedule for implementation, based on the timelines you have been given 
    
*   document the costs, ensuring the budget is met
    
*   document the resources that will be required for implementation (consider human resources and physical resources, you may like to develop an action plan or similar).
    

If you are completing the case study business, you will meet with your assessor to discuss training needs. You can develop your documents in any format you like (for example, you may have access to a project management tool in which you may like to develop your schedule, or you can use a spreadsheet or word processing application). If you are completing this task in your chosen business, you can take notes about the training needs. You do not need to record and submit your discussion with this person. Complete Section 5 of your Project Portfolio.Submit your Portfolio to your assessor for approval.

**8\. Finalisation.**
You are now to complete Section 6 of your _Project Portfolio,_ in which you will finalise your work.You will need to submit seek feedback from relevant personnel (if you are completing the case study, this will be your assessor, who will play the roles of the CEO and the Academic Manager). Request that feedback and/or sign off is provided in writing. Review the feedback and if you are required to make changes, do so accordingly. Submit your changes and again seek sign off. Complete Section 6 of your Project Portfolio.Submit your Portfolio to your assessor for approval.

**9\. Submit your completed Project Portfolio.** 
Make sure you have completed all sections of your Project Portfolio, answered all questions, provided enough detail as indicated and proofread for spelling and grammar as necessary. Make sure all required documentation and recordings are part of your submission. Submit your work to your assessor for marking.
