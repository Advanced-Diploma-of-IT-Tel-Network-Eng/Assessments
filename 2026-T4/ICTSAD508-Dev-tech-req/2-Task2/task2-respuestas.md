- King Edward VII College is a Registered Training Organisation (RTO) established in 2010 and based in Melbourne's CBD. This college is nationally recognized for providing high-quality training in management, marketing, human resources, and international business. The college adheres to the national VET Quality Framework to maintain its standards and through a continuous improvement system to keep in compliance with quality.

It currently has approximately 500 students and 24 employees.
