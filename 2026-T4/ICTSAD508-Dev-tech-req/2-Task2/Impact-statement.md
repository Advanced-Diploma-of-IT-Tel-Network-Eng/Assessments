# Impact Statement


## Suitable Software Solutions
- Google Workspace: Staff will transition to working entirely in the cloud (email, documents, and shared storage), with access from any campus and role-based access control. They will receive basic training on how to use the suite.
- Backup and Disaster Recovery: Backups will become automatic and managed by Google in the cloud. Storage will now be in the cloud, so information will always be accessible and recoverable from the internet.
- Moodle Cloud Upgrade: The Moodle Cloud plan will be upgraded with the same provider, without migration. Therefore, there will be no impact.
- New Website Design and Features: The new site on TALKK CMS is accessible (WCAG 2.2) and multilingual, and integrates with Moodle via APIs. Therefore, it will now be necessary to create website materials compatible with the new accessibility options. Students and visitors will now be able to find and enjoy these new features. The domain remains the same, so there is no impact on the URL.
- New marketing tools (TALKK CMS): Marketing campaigns and content are managed from the TALKK CMS and sent directly via APIs. The Marketing team will receive specific training from the provider.

## Hardware and networking solutions
- New Laptops: Staff receive new, powerful ThinkPads with a direct warranty from the provider and the College's secure configuration already applied. They are faster, lighter, run the new software smoothly, and are designed for a long lifespan. Therefore, staff may take some time to adapt to the change, as well as to the new operating system.
- Classroom devices: These improve audio and video quality in blended and online classes, which is a known issue. Trainers will need a short training session to use them.
- New ultra-fast internet features: Each campus has Telstra nbn ultra-fast with redundant connections. Internet access is now faster and with low latency. This has a positive impact on the College's new cloud-based, online model.
- Network devices (firewalls, switches, Wi-Fi 7): New, segmented LAN and Wi-Fi networks across all campuses. Staff, students, and visitors connect to separate, secure networks.

## Security protocols
- Data encryption: Data will always be encrypted using HTTPS/TLS and an IPSec VPN. There is no impact on the user.
- MFA and access security: All staff will require MFA as their login method. They will receive a notification to update their password and authentication method. This will impact how staff log in to Google Workspace.
- Always VPN on to work: To access internal systems from off-campus, staff must connect to the College's VPN. This will impact all devices from which staff connect.
- Endpoint protection: All computers have FortiClient installed, managed by IT. Staff cannot disable it and must accept security updates when they are released. This will impact the sites that staff can visit.
- Security certificates: The website's SSL/TLS certificates will be the responsibility of TALKK, Google, and MoodleCloud, so staff no longer need to worry about renewing them. IT only manages them on the College's own systems. This impacts the IT team's functions.
- Web filtering: Staff may notice that many sites are blocked from the College network, either by the firewall or the VPN. For example, news sites, gambling sites, adult content sites, and games are blocked. If any site is blocked in error, it must be reported to support.

## Change process
- API integration process: The connection between Moodle and the website depends on APIs. Any connection or compatibility issues are reported to IT, which coordinates with the Developer during the project and with technical support for the following 6 months.
- IT support process: Each provider supports their own service, and staff contact them directly. This impacts how IT support is managed.
- License renewals and purchasing: The first year is paid for. From the second year onward, Finance manages renewals directly with each provider, following the Purchasing Policy and Procedures.
- Marketing workflow: Website content and campaigns are published from the TALKK CMS. TALKK reviews compliance with WCAG 2.2, which impacts the content that is published.
- Staff training and security awareness: All staff across all campuses receive basic cybersecurity training, and each department receives training on its specific tools.

## The impact of changes on the business's value and supply chain

### Value Chain:
- Marketing: An accessible, multilingual website helps attract more students; 10% growth is expected.
- Delivery: With a modernized Moodle, all courses can be offered online and in a blended format, with improved quality.
- Support Activities: The scalable infrastructure allows for the opening of the Sydney and Brisbane campuses, staff work more efficiently, and data is protected.

### Supply Chain:
- The College now relies on several providers (Google, MoodleCloud, TALKK, Fortinet, Lenovo, Ubiquiti, Telstra).
- The SLA sets prices and covers delivery to any campus, reducing cost risks during expansion.
- As a risk, dependence on third parties increases; therefore, SLAs and renewals must be monitored from the second year onward.
