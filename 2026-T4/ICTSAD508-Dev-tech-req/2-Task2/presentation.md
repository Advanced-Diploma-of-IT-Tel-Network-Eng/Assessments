## Slide 5 - Legislation, standards and procedures (2:00)

# Legislation, Standards and Procedures

### Legislation & Standards
- **Privacy Act 1988:** Australian Privacy Principles (APPs); access control, encryption, data breach response plan
- **Copyright Act 1968:** Protection of learning materials and website content
- **Work Health and Safety Act 2011:** Hardware installation standards and ergonomic workstations
- **Fair Work Act 2009:** Staff system usage policies and operational training
- **Spam Act 2003:** Marketing email compliance and campaign rules
- **Australian Consumer Law & Fair Trading Act 2012 (Vic):** Accurate course details and fair advertising
- **WCAG 2.2 (Level A):** Web accessibility compliance for main site and Moodle
- **VET Quality Framework:** Operational alignment with College Strategic & Operational Plan

### Internal Procedures
- **Purchasing Policy and Procedures:** Formal quotes and Quotation Record Form execution
- **Risk Management System:** Continuous improvement and mitigation protocols
- **SDLC & Change Management:** Systems Development Life Cycle governance
- **Information & Cyber Security:** Data backup, disaster recovery, asset register, and network diagrams

## Slide 7 - Technical specifications: Solution 1, ICT Infrastructure and Security (2:00)

# Technical Specifications: Solution 1 (ICT & Security)

| Area | Technical Specification |
|---|---|
| **Hardware** | Workstations, laptops, classroom devices; cloud or dedicated servers; firewalls, routers, switches, access points; redundant network hardware; cloud and offsite backup storage |
| **Software** | Operating systems and licences; cloud/on-premise productivity suites; Identity and Access Management (RBAC, MFA); endpoint protection; monitoring/logging; backup and recovery software |
| **Network** | High-bandwidth ISP connection with redundancy; WAN/VPN (IPSec) connecting campuses; segmented private and public LAN/Wi-Fi |
| **Interface** | Detailed network infrastructure diagrams and ICT infrastructure mapping |

### Security Measures
- Strong passwords, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Firewalls, routine software patching, encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS, IPSec) and at rest
- Active network monitoring, routine backup testing, and Privacy Act data breach response workflow
